# Design Quality rubric interpretation

A score of 92+ means the experience is release-level *under this internal rubric*, not an objective claim of being the world's best site.

High score characteristics:
- design choices form a coherent POV;
- page rhythm has deliberate contrast and pause;
- typography and imagery carry brand meaning;
- components do not expose template origin;
- interaction/motion clarify rather than distract;
- mobile feels authored;
- accessibility/performance constraints have shaped—not merely followed—the design.

The category floors prevent a polished visual layer from hiding weak UX, responsiveness or other critical dimensions. Hard gates remain separate from the score.
