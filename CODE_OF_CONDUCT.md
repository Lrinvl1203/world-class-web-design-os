# Community conduct

Web Design OS is an open design and engineering project. Participation should make it easier for people with different backgrounds, abilities, identities, and levels of experience to contribute useful work.

## Expected

- Critique the work and evidence, not the person.
- Be specific about observed behavior, constraints, and tradeoffs.
- Credit references and collaborators.
- Treat accessibility and inclusive participation as quality requirements.
- Accept correction and repair harm promptly.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Plagiarism or presenting another creator's work as original.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

To report conduct concerns privately, use the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and label the subject “Community conduct.” Reports will be handled as privately as practical; conflicts of interest should be disclosed so another reviewer can handle the report.
