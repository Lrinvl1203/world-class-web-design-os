# Example Project Brief

## Business
Boutique hospitality digital concierge.

## Audience
Domestic and international guests using a phone during arrival, stay, and checkout.

## Primary job
Find the right stay information in seconds without contacting the host.

## Primary actions
Check-in, entry instructions, Wi-Fi, appliance help, checkout, emergency contact.

## Brand character
Warm architectural calm; premium but not ostentatious; urban retreat; high legibility.

## Anti-direction
Not a Linktree, not a SaaS dashboard, not a generic hotel template, not card-heavy.

## References
Three screenshots selected for typography, editorial composition, and restrained motion. Analyze rather than copy.

## Constraints
Mobile-first, Korean/English, fast on cellular, no essential information hidden behind animation.
